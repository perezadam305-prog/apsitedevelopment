Common questions

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Send photos for a quick quote, or we can come look at the job. No charge, no pressure.

How fast can you come out?

Junk removal is usually within the same week, often in 1–2 days. Dig and grading jobs depend on the size; we'll give you a date when we quote.

How much does junk removal cost?

It's priced by how much space your stuff takes up in the truck and what it is. Send a photo and we'll give you a price right away.

My yard floods every time it rains. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Most yards need a French drain, some regrading, or both so water runs away from the house. Send us a photo of where the water sits.

Do you work with contractors?

Yes. We sub out excavator and skid steer work with an operator: base prep, trenching, demo cleanup and haul-off, by the day or by the job.

What areas do you cover?

All of Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties.
